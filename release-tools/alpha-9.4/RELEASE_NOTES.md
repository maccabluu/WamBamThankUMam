# Wam Bam Alpha 9.4.0

Four new levels use all ten supplied icons: red lips, pink bow, leopard heel,
hand mirror, gold earrings, microphone, rose compact, pink drink, nails and
eyelashes. Each new level has its own icon palette and collection targets.

- Level 6: Lipstick & Leopard. Collect lips, bows and heels.
- Level 7: Spotlight Disco. Collect microphones, compacts and activate Discos.
- Level 8: Glamour Vault. Clear handbags and collect nails and earrings.
- Level 9: Bam Grand Finale. Collect lips, microphones and activate Discos.

The scrolling map includes target previews, a level list, a My Level button,
replay, earned stars, clear locks and a Play Next button. Completed levels save
their best star rating and score. Stars depend on moves left: 30% earns three,
10% earns two, and completing the targets earns one. Older saves retain their
unlocked levels. Earlier completions without a saved rating show a completion
tick until replayed.

Match 4 for a line blast, match a T or L for an area blast, and match 5 or a
2 by 2 square for a Disco. Swap specials together for larger clears. Boosters
now count towards collection targets. Swaps lock input until resolution,
invalid swaps return without costing moves, falling pieces move together,
handbags stop pieces passing through, and dead boards reshuffle into a playable
layout. Win and loss checks also run after booster and Disco actions.

This update uses the supplied Alpha 9.3.0 APK as its base. The 6 by 10 board,
gameplay background, original home screen, events, audio settings, package name
and saved progress remain supported. Settings shows the installed 9.4.0 version.

Android package: `com.macca.wambamthankumam`. Version code: `94000`.
The build verifies the original signing certificate so the APK installs as an
update. Screen checks cover phone and tablet layouts. Physical device testing
is still needed for device-specific performance and audio behaviour.
