# Wam Bam Alpha 7.9.0 — Events & Navigation

## New events

- Daily Spotlight gives one free coin reward each day.
- Match Mania tracks 25 cleared icons across every level.
- Couture Rescue tracks four cleared handbags in Level 5.
- Completed events award saved coins that also appear on Home and during gameplay.
- Players can enable an Android local reminder from the Events screen.

## Fixed and improved

- Road Map level balls are smaller and centred inside the artwork's gold bases.
- The Road Map still scrolls upward automatically as new levels unlock.
- Returning Home from gameplay or the Road Map restores the complete Home artwork instead of showing a black screen.
- The Home character movement uses a tighter character crop and the wink now lands on the eye.
- Level 5 locked and open handbags stay inside their board tiles.
- Background music and active sound effects pause on Android pause, background, page-hide and app-close events.

## Android package

- Package: `com.macca.wambamthankumam`
- Version: `7.9.0` (`70900`)
- Android notification permission is requested only when the player enables event reminders.
