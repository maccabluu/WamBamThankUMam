# Wam Bam Alpha 9.0.0

Alpha 9.0 introduces a clearer, larger gameplay board across Levels 1–5.

## Changes

- Changed every gameplay board to 4 icons across by 6 icons down.
- Enlarged the gameplay icons while keeping square cells and smooth movement.
- Centred the 4 × 6 board inside the parchment frame on every level.
- Updated match detection, swaps, drops, cascades and reshuffles for the new dimensions.
- Updated Lip Laser, Heel Smash, Disco Ball and Swap behaviour for the 4 × 6 board.
- Repositioned all Level 5 handbag blockers within the new board.
- Kept the updated background with the Coins and Hearts gameplay tabs removed.
- Kept Moves and all live targets aligned with the upper panels.
