# Wam Bam Alpha 7.8.0 — Couture Vault

## New

- Level 5: a new neon Wam Bam handbag boutique and Couture Vault theme.
- Eight chained handbag blockers across the Level 5 board.
- First adjacent match opens a locked handbag; the next adjacent match clears it.
- Handbag stages award coins, and cleared handbags count toward the Level 5 target.
- Road Map progression and automatic upward scrolling now continue through Level 5.

## Fixed and improved

- Home coins, lives and level numbers now fit inside their artwork.
- The Home wink animation is aligned to the character's eye.
- Settings labels and buttons fit without clipped or wrapped text.
- Road Map level balls are resized to sit inside their gold pads.
- Level 5 uses the same smooth swap, match, cascade and drop timing as the earlier levels.
- The APK keeps the existing `com.macca.wambamthankumam` package and signing identity for update installs.

## Level 5 blocker rules

1. Make a match of three or more directly above, below, left or right of a chained handbag.
2. The first hit removes the chains and opens the handbag.
3. Make another adjacent match to clear the handbag and reduce the handbag target.

The Lip Laser and Heel Smash boosters can also damage handbag stages.
